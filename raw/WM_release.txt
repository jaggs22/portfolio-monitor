“WM
delivered another quarter of solid operating and financial performance, in line with our expectations, underscoring the consistent execution
of our strategy,” said Jim Fish, WM’s CEO. “Adjusted
operating EBITDA increased 5.8% in the third quarter driven by strong price-to-cost spread in Collection and Disposal and growing contributions
from our healthcare and sustainability businesses. Notably, Healthcare Solutions revenue grew 3.7% in the quarter and operating EBITDA
increased more than 30%, driven by effective cost management, synergy capture, and pricing growth. Overall, our results reflect the earnings
power of our diversified business and our ability to consistently create value for shareholders.”(a)


 


Fish continued, “The quality of our earnings
is evident in the strength of our cash flow generation. Net cash flow from operations increased 12% through the first nine months of
the year, reflecting disciplined operational execution and continued strong returns on investments that are improving productivity, enhancing
service and supporting sustainable long-term growth. We are affirming our 2026 guidance ranges, as we remain on track to meet our financial
objectives.”


 


 


KEY
HIGHLIGHTS FOR the Third quarter OF 2026 


 


•Total
 Company revenue grew 6.3%, driven by core price of 5.8% and Collection and Disposal yield
 of 3.4%. In addition to disciplined execution on pricing, revenue growth was driven by increased
 energy surcharges and higher volumes in the recycling and renewable energy businesses as
 a result of completed growth projects.(e)(f)


•Collection
 and Disposal volume declined 0.4%, or declined 0.5% on a workday adjusted basis. Volumes
 continued to be impacted by the Company’s intentional shedding of lower-margin residential
 business and commercial declines largely related to prior national account broker losses.


•Operating
 expenses were 59.8% of revenue, compared to 58.9% in the prior year. Operating efficiency
 in the Collection and Disposal business drove a 40-basis point improvement, which was more
 than offset by dilutive impacts of 130 basis points primarily related to higher fuel costs and increased risk management costs.


 





 

 
 

 
 


 





•SG&A
 expenses were 9.6% of revenue, or 9.3% on an adjusted basis, an improvement of 70 basis points
 both on a reported and adjusted basis from the prior year, reflecting strong cost discipline
 and continued synergy capture in Healthcare Solutions.(a)


•Favorable
 price-to-cost spread in the Collection and Disposal business contributed 40 basis points
 to adjusted operating EBITDA margin, and Healthcare Solutions operating performance added
 another 30 basis points. These contributions were more than offset by 40 basis points
 related to higher fuel costs and 40 basis points related to increased risk management
 costs. Overall, adjusted operating EBITDA margin compressed by 10 basis points
 compared to the prior year period. (a)


•Through
 the first nine months of the year, the Company generated $4.87 billion of net cash provided
 by operating activities. The 12.0% increase from the prior year was driven by earnings growth
 and favorable working capital.


•Through
 the first nine months of the year, free cash flow was $3.01 billion, compared to $2.11 billion
 in the prior year period, an increase of more than 42%. Conversion of adjusted operating
 EBITDA to free cash flow was 50% in the first nine months of the year.(a)


•The
 Company returned $957 million to shareholders in the third quarter, consisting of $580 million
 in share repurchases and $377 million in cash dividends, bringing returns to shareholders
 in the first nine months of the year to more than $2.72 billion, which is more than 90% of
 free cash flow generated during the period and reflects the Company’s commitment to
 harvesting the benefits of its investments. Through the first nine months of the year, the
 Company repurchased $1.58 billion of its shares and continues to expect to repurchase a total
 of $2 billion of its shares in 2026.


•During
 the quarter, the Company began operations at a new renewable natural gas facility in New
 York, marking its eighteenth new facility completed since 2022.


•The
 Company expects to achieve its full-year financial guidance, with revenue expected
 to be at the high end of the guidance range of $26.275 to $26.475 billion, adjusted operating
 EBITDA to be within the guidance range of $8.15 to $8.25 billion, and free cash flow to be
 at the high end of the guidance range of $3.75 to $3.85 billion.(a)

about wm


 


WM (WM.com)
is North America's leading provider of comprehensive environmental solutions. Previously known as Waste Management and based in Houston,
Texas, WM is driven by commitments to put people first and achieve success with integrity. WM, through its subsidiaries, provides collection,
recycling and disposal services to millions of residential, commercial, industrial, medical and municipal customers throughout the U.S.
and Canada. With innovative infrastructure and capabilities in recycling, organics and renewable energy, WM provides environmental solutions
to and collaborates with its customers in helping them pursue their sustainability goals. In North America, WM has the largest disposal
network and collection fleet, is the largest recycler and is a leader in beneficial use of landfill gas, with a growing network of renewable
natural gas plants and the most landfill gas-to-electricity plants, as well as the largest heavy-duty natural gas truck fleet in the
industry. WM, through its subsidiaries, also provides collection and disposal services of regulated medical waste and secure information
destruction services in the U.S., Canada and Western Europe. To learn more about WM and the company's sustainability progress and solutions,
visit Sustainability.WM.com.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures


 


To supplement its financial information, the Company
has presented, and/or may discuss on the conference call, adjusted measures including adjusted earnings per diluted share, adjusted net
income, adjusted income from operations and margin, adjusted operating EBITDA and margin, adjusted operating expense and margin, and
adjusted SG&A expenses and margin. All adjusted measures and free cash flow are non-GAAP financial measures, as defined in Regulation
G of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended. The Company reports its financial results in compliance with GAAP but believes
that also discussing non-GAAP measures provides investors with (i) financial measures the Company uses in the management of its business
and (ii) additional, meaningful comparisons of current results to prior periods’ results by excluding items that the Company does
not believe reflect its fundamental business performance and are not representative or indicative of its results of operations.


 


 


The Company discusses free cash flow and provides
a projection of free cash flow because the Company believes that it is indicative of its ability to pay its quarterly dividends, repurchase
common stock, fund acquisitions and other investments and, in the absence of refinancings, to repay its debt obligations. The Company
believes free cash flow gives investors useful insight into how the Company views its liquidity, but the use of free cash flow as a liquidity
measure has material limitations because it excludes certain expenditures that are required or that the Company has committed to, such
as declared dividend payments and debt service requirements. The Company defines free cash flow as net cash provided by operating activities,
less capital expenditures, plus proceeds from divestitures of businesses and other assets (net of cash divested); this definition may
not be comparable to similarly-titled measures reported by other companies.


 


The quantitative reconciliations of non-GAAP measures
to the most comparable GAAP measures are included in the accompanying schedules, with the exception of projected adjusted operating EBITDA
and margin. Non-GAAP measures should not be considered a substitute for financial measures presented in accordance with GAAP.
